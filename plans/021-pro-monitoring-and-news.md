# Plan 021: Pro monitoring, incremental news, and measured event forecasts

Status: COUNCIL-APPROVED STAGED PLAN (3 rounds; all final reviewers satisfied). User authorized implementation on 2026-11-11. A–C implemented locally, default-off; no production deployment/activation. D–F remain gated. See [shadow operations](021-shadow-operations.md) and [execution contract](021-execution-contract.md).
Baseline: main `1bc9035`, 2026-11-11. Separate uncommitted ambient/Eve 1.0 RC migration exists in this worktree; it is not deployed and is not silently included in this plan's implementation.

## Outcome and scope

Use Vercel Pro to notice changes sooner while preserving Poof's deterministic authority over money. Start with frequent observation, then separately enable mechanical exits and at most two scheduled full reviews per market day. Record news forecasts prospectively; do not promote them into trading signals merely because a model sounds confident.

The account is a small, live, long-only Trading 212 ISA. VUAG is the default investment and benchmark. No-trade is valid. Faster checks do not establish alpha, and withholding stock entries does not remove the market exposure already held in VUAG.

This is a staged roadmap, not one large PR. Phase 0 is readiness and a written execution-contract design only. The first code slice is A: an isolated calendar plus shadow-only position observations. Phase 0 execution requirements are implemented later in D, not in A-C. Later live phases require their own reviewed change and explicit activation. No Alpaca, shorting, leverage, paid feed purchase, broad framework rewrite, extra grading agent, or automatic sale of VUAG based on a macro forecast. Deposit handling and actual-trade replay remain separate backlog work. Plan 019's rank-IC study is referenced in the vault but has no plan file here; do not claim it is implemented.

## Evidence from the current code

- `agent/lib/state.ts`: `newPositionsToday` resets to zero across cycles. A second full cycle can evade the nominal four-new-positions daily cap.
- `agent/lib/funnel-schedule.ts` and `convex/memory.ts:claimFunnelChunk`: four chunks claimed once per UTC date. Extra cron fires do not refresh the universe.
- `agent/tools/manage_positions.ts:exitIntentKey`: one exit per ticker per ET day, explicitly assuming once-daily execution. Re-entry, partial fills, or a residual position need revised semantics.
- `agent/lib/orders.ts`: BUY intent written before send; SELL bookkeeping after send; placement helpers can throw, but `evaluateAndExecute` returns an `outcome unknown` rejection and stops the batch. Core wrappers currently collapse some send errors into `failed`; recovery must inspect returned results as well as exceptions. Existing query-then-write intent checks are not an account-wide concurrent-run lock.
- `agent/tools/record_cycle.ts` sweeps cash into VUAG. It is NOT a read-only journaling tool. A monitor must never call it or the core funding/sweep routines.
- `review_performance` already uses `thesisBreakCheck`; `get_news` and the funnel already screen catalysts with Jev. Reuse those definitions; avoid another equivalent classifier.
- `coreFundingKey` is ET-date-based, despite per-cycle wording: funding can suppress sweeping for the rest of the day. VUAG is London-listed; the proposed afternoon US review is after its regular session.
- `defaultIntentKey` includes ET date, ticker, side and notional: identical same-day top-ups/SELLs can be suppressed while different notionals bypass that marker. Funnel scan claims currently use UTC dates; execution uses ET dates.
- `resolveRiskState` writes account peaks and day references; `manage_positions` also writes live peaks and observed prices. These are not shadow-safe readers. Accepted SELL results currently flow to full-close bookkeeping without proof that the broker is flat.
- `clock.ts` covers DST and full-day holidays, but not exchange early closes. Daily candles from Tiingo cannot retrospectively validate five-minute exits or one-hour forecasts.
- The vault deliberately limited observability expansion after repeated false accusations and capture defects. Add only the records needed to make this change safe and testable.

## Target cadence (not yet active)

- Position observation: every five minutes during the actual US regular session.
- News ingestion: every fifteen minutes during that session, held stocks plus at most twelve shortlisted symbols. Keep the existing broad daily scan initially.
- Full decisions: retain the existing 15:00 UTC cycle until live readiness is met; later replace it with 10:00 and 14:00 America/New_York slots, at most two scheduled starts per ET day. A closed/early-close afternoon slot is skipped, never backfilled after the close.
- Reports: retain current per-cycle reporting until a separate presentation change is reviewed; monitors stay silent except deduplicated material alerts and failures. Do not silently weaken the always-report trading contract in this work.

UTC cron ticks are only wakeups. An isolated pure exchange-session resolver supplies ET session date, open/close, holidays and early closes from a versioned NYSE calendar table (https://www.nyse.com/trade/hours-calendars). A-C do not replace the current live calendar, catch-up or watchdog. F additionally needs a London-session resolver with UK holidays/early closes for VUAG; US and UK DST transition weeks are tested separately. It decides due slots, so DST changes and UTC-day boundaries cannot change the trading day. No cron expression alone encodes market hours.

## Phase 0: readiness and execution contract

1. Verify installed/locked dependencies on Node 24, both typechecks, unit tests and bundle smoke. Decide explicitly whether implementation targets current main or the separately reviewed Eve upgrade; use that version's bundled guides. `convex/_generated/ai/guidelines.md` is currently absent: locate the project's current Convex guidance before implementation; do not invent APIs.
2. Specify the exchange-session resolver, built in A: holidays, early closes, authoritative source, maintenance year and stale-calendar behavior. Outside supported dates A-C record `skipped: calendar-unsupported` and alert; D-F modes refuse to start. The legacy daily cycle, catch-up and watchdog keep their current calendar until F explicitly replaces it.
3. Design one durable account-execution owner shared by full cycles, catch-up, Slack-triggered execution, mechanical exits, core funding and core sweep. Key by environment plus broker account, not Slack session. Include owner ID, generation, lease and heartbeat.
4. Acquire ownership in a Convex mutation before dispatching any execution-capable work and carry its identity through the agent session to every money-changing tool. Recheck ownership immediately at the actual physical HTTP send boundary, including every 429/backoff retry and precision retry; reject stale owners. Recheck market-session eligibility after waits and on every retry. After an order POST is dispatched, a 429, timeout or lost response is an UNKNOWN outcome: do not send another order POST for that logical operation. D removes generic 429/backoff retries for order writes; read requests may still back off. A precision retry is permitted only after a definitive broker rejection proving the prior POST placed nothing. A core funding-marker failure before send-start is a definitive no-order; a core `failed` after send-start remains unknown. Bound physical request/retry deadlines, including core orders; an outer tool-entry check is insufficient. Keep the lease for the actual session/tool lifetime, not merely until the schedule's `send` returns. Observation does not take execution ownership, but it cannot make competing Trading 212 reads; broker writes are serialized.
5. Lease expiry is NOT proof that an external order was never sent. A crash after send, timeout or ambiguous outcome puts that logical operation into reconciliation-required state. Do not automatically reassign the account for trading until the old worker is quiescent and orders/positions/history have established the outcome. A generation check alone cannot fence a broker that accepts no fencing token. Stock orders, core funding and core sweep share this send/reconciliation state; `failed` after send-start is UNKNOWN, never proof of no effect. Current code has no order-history client: fresh pending-order/position reads may prove a fill but an unchanged quantity and no pending order do not prove no send. If broker evidence cannot settle it, remain unknown; a new read-only status/history adapter would need its own verified API contract. Automatic takeover requires a proven bound on the old physical sender/retry lifetime, explicit cancellation/drain confirmation, and repeated consistent broker reads. Workflow resumption and deployment replacement invalidate a naive platform-duration timer. If quiescence cannot be proved, no automatic takeover. Preserve a documented read-only/operator recovery path; never promise exactly-once broker effects.
6. Reserve daily entry capacity atomically before sending a BUY that opens a new position. Key by account, environment and ET trading date. The single reservation ledger is the authoritative daily counter, using `resolveLimits().maxNewPositionsPerDay` (default four), never a new hardcoded cap. It covers all callers and same-day re-entries after flat, excludes the VUAG core, counts each logical entry once, retains capacity for pending/unknown sends, and releases only definitive no-order outcomes, including a precision skip proven to have placed no order. Do not reuse the current permanently retained BUY marker as the capacity counter; migrating a marker must retain its protective meaning. Top-ups still obey per-name/deployed limits but do not masquerade as new distinct positions. Existing exposure, pending orders and reservations must be considered together.
7. Replace daily-only exit markers with durable position-generation/logical-exit identities and explicit pending/partial/completed/unknown outcomes. Reconcile actual remaining quantity before another send. Re-entering the same symbol creates a new position generation. Never fix this by dropping deduplication or blindly retrying a partial exit. Accepted is not filled: do not call the existing full-close helper or book whole-position P&L until confirmed flat. A residual retains the same generation, entry time, live peak and exit levels; realized P&L is based on confirmed fills and remains unknown when evidence is unavailable. A subsequent residual sell is permitted only after the prior send outcome is known. Cover ordinary top-ups and discretionary SELLs too: a logical decision ID deduplicates retries, while a genuinely new same-day decision has a distinct ID and still passes account, capacity and exposure checks.
8. Define how separate same-day cycle records, traces, memory edits and weekly summaries retain turn identity and do not overwrite or double-count a day. Do not rewrite stored history or silently turn all cycles into daily aggregates.

9. Freeze risk sampling semantics in the D design. A-C never call `resolveRiskState` or `saveRiskState`. E reads the existing halt state without refreshing account equity peaks/day references; its denser position peaks are separate from account-level peaks. F deliberately adds a second full-cycle account-risk observation, which may tighten a drawdown brake even with identical percentages. Capture a parallel shadow comparison and disclose this behavioral change before F; do not reset day-start references mid-day.
10. Preserve the legacy live path during A-C. For E/F, normal BUYs, SELLs and core legs all require verified ownership at every send; an owner/intent error causes the NEW high-frequency path to skip, never fall through to the current SELL fail-open branch. There is a real policy conflict: legacy unconditional SELL-on-Convex-error and strict multi-writer exclusion cannot both be promised. D must not silently remove the legacy fallback. E/F remain BLOCKED until a separately reviewed, explicitly approved risk-policy decision permits coordination failure to block a SELL, or a demonstrated alternative preserves that fallback without concurrent sends. This plan approves neither changing that policy nor activating E/F. An independent broker/operator exit procedure remains available; never advertise five-minute checks as guaranteed protection.

Phase 0 artifacts must specify the transaction/state transitions before implementation. No change to numerical risk limits, broker-total authority, required model reasoning, or observer isolation.

## Phase 1: shadow position monitor (first runnable slice)

Create a bounded scheduled handler calling a read-only position-observation service directly, without a root agent/model turn. A adds NO new Trading 212 reads. Start from an existing durable, confirmed position snapshot plus stored generation/entry/peak/exit levels, and poll timestamped Finnhub quotes for those known holdings. Persist the snapshot identity/age and feed identity with every observation; require a coherent snapshot no older than one US trading session, reject missing quantity/entry/generation data, and censor immediately on any known closure or generation change. If current durable capture cannot supply that input, A records `skipped: position-state-unavailable` and limits itself to calendar/quote collection until the capability gap is addressed. Compute hypothetical exits only from qualified shadow inputs. This is an observational study of known holdings, not a current broker-portfolio audit; it must not claim live exit readiness. Fresh broker reads are a D-or-later capability after shared read admission is proved.

The shadow service receives an explicit capability allowlist: stored confirmed-position/trade reads, timestamped market-data reads, and dedicated shadow-table writes only. No Trading 212 client is passed to A. It must not reuse `manage_positions`/`review_performance` as a supposedly read-only wrapper. Specifically, it cannot call `resolveRiskState`, `saveRiskState`, live observed-price writes or any generic `Memory` mutation method. In addition, it has no place-order, close-trade, save-live-peak, amend-memory, dispatch-agent, core-funding or core-sweep capability. Invoke the actual handler against fakes that throw on ANY unexpected external call. Shadow peak sampling is separate: five-minute peak ratchets change strategy behavior even when exit constants are unchanged. Do not write shadow peaks back to live trades.

Run ten complete market sessions for operational evidence, not proof of profitability. Reconcile expected ticks against successful/failed/skipped observations, quote freshness, provider 429s, costs, and the reason each hypothetical exit differs from the daily baseline. Use the same collected observation tape for the intraday and daily-sampled comparator; censor a position after actual live closure and label missing/stale intervals. This is a mechanical trigger comparison, not a counterfactual whole-portfolio P&L simulation. No intraday backtest claims from daily OHLC, no hand-picked winning examples.

Proposed operating budgets: at most eighty position observations and twenty-seven news polls per normal session; at most twelve watchlist symbols plus the held stock sleeve; early-close tick budgets/denominators scale to the actual session duration; one shared request budget across Trading 212, Finnhub and Tiingo, measured by endpoint, pagination and retries, not just top-level ticks. Trading 212 cash/portfolio reads are rate-limited and caches/backoff are per isolate. A cannot consume Trading 212 capacity at all. For Finnhub/Tiingo, validate quota headroom after existing work, use fixed exclusion windows around the broad funnel/full reviews, no monitor retry and stop on the first 429; any observed degradation to the live path disables shadow polling. Before D-or-later introduces broker shadow reads, implement and test cross-process read admission that reserves live headroom. A best-effort session activity signal is only a hint, never authority to admit a broker read. If added later, its writers cover cycle/catch-up/Slack session starts and ends with TTL; missing/expired/unreadable state means possibly active. Writers are bounded, catch/log-only and cannot change live orders/reports when they fail. Test the unscheduled Slack-run race between signal-check and read; if exclusion cannot be proved, new broker reads remain disabled. This source restriction removes the nonexistent activity-signal writer from A rather than silently adding it to the live path. Reserve provider capacity for the established daily cycle; A-C cannot consume its headroom on a best-effort basis. Quotas are checked against the actual active Finnhub/Tiingo tiers before enabling. Brokerage quote freshness and API timestamps must be established in the spike; if unavailable, record an inability to validate freshness rather than guessing. HTTP completion time is not a quote timestamp. For new intraday exits, require a validated timestamped execution-price source, quote age <=60 seconds and cash/position snapshot skew <=1 second (initial conservative eligibility values, validated against the source contract). If current T212 data cannot meet that requirement, E is a no-go; do not substitute a Finnhub price into broker sizing without a separate reviewed change. These conditions do not alter the existing once-daily path in A-C.

On a timeout, stale/incomplete snapshot or failed read, record an unknown/skipped observation, never manufacture an empty portfolio or a clean bill of health. Suppress repeated identical alerts. Existing daily trading remains the production authority.

## Phase 2: incremental news observation

Poll existing REST sources initially. A stream/webhook is an optional future substitution only after verifying availability, permissions, source freshness and cost on the actual subscription; no new queue service is required initially.

Reuse existing Jev screening questions and thesis-break checker, preserving model/prompt versions and their distinction from directional forecasts. Deduplicate by source event ID and canonical story identity; syndicated copies of one story are one independent event, not repeated evidence. A corrected story is a versioned update, not an unrelated forecast. Only genuinely new canonical stories incur new screening/forecast calls; cap new forecast attempts at twenty per ET day, with every over-budget event recorded as not attempted. Treat headlines/articles as untrusted data, never scheduling or tool instructions.

Persist source publication time, first-seen time, source identity, canonical URL, referenced symbols, classification and versions. First-seen time is the earliest possible decision time. Incremental ingestion uses a durable cursor plus a bounded overlap window; advance the cursor only after durable processing. A missed poll catches up from the cursor within a capped lookback; old events are explicitly stale and cannot trigger immediate action. For the initial forecast cohort, publication-to-first-seen delay must be <=15 minutes, and publication time must be trustworthy. Overnight/pre-market events first ingested at the opening poll, retrospective catch-up beyond that delay and unknown publication times remain archived/classified but are ineligible for prospective scoring. Freeze the at-most-twelve-symbol shortlist from the existing daily funnel output at the first ET-session poll; changes to held positions are versioned from confirmed records. Freeze this eligible held-stock/shortlist universe before each poll; the model cannot select which event-symbol pairs receive cohort rows.

Thesis-break alerts show evidence and remain advisory. No news handler calls submit_orders, manage_positions, record_cycle, amend_memory or an execution-capable agent. Rate/cost exhaustion degrades news coverage visibly and leaves the existing trading path running. Do not steal the broker/provider budget needed for exits.

## Phase 3: prospectively scored event forecasts

The first experiment explicitly narrows “market swings” to event-linked single-stock relative returns. Macro/index direction is a separate research gate below; neither result authorizes trading.

Preregister the horizon as the close of the first FULL US trading session whose open is strictly after forecast commitment (Friday commitments target Monday close absent a holiday). This avoids ambiguous last-minute same-day predictions. Freeze eligible event-symbol pairs before model inference. Record source publication, first-seen/ingestion, input cutoff, model completion and immutable forecast commit times separately. The model receives only inputs at/before the recorded cutoff; no outcomes or later story revisions.

After forecast commitment, obtain the first valid timestamped stock/proxy quote pair, each timestamp >=commit time, within sixty seconds of commitment and <=sixty seconds old, with pair skew <=five seconds. Both prices must precede the target session's open. A missing/late pair makes the row unscoreable; never backdate the forecast or use a pre-model price as its executable start. Propose Finnhub `getQuote` for held and shortlisted US symbols plus SPY, subject to validating the actual subscription's quote timestamps/freshness in the spike; receipt time cannot replace its quote time. Prediction probability means the stock's total USD return exceeds the specified broad-market proxy's total USD return between that start pair and the target close. Probability/abstention, prompt/model version, cutoff, cohort and baseline IDs are immutable. SPY is research-only and is not an ISA order candidate. This target is distinct from VUAG/GBP account alpha. If first valid quotes cannot be acquired in this contract, no Phase 3 score is produced.

Do not score a forecast without a valid starting observation. Score using consistent adjusted-price semantics for splits/dividends; do not mix raw starting quotes and adjusted closing prices without a documented conversion. Missing/delayed prices yield pending/unscoreable outcomes, not losses or wins. Preserve every eligible event, abstention and failed prediction attempt so selection bias is visible.

The target uses the resolver's actual close, including early closes. Horizon length varies across weekends/holidays; compare frozen baselines within preregistered horizon buckets. Report ineligible and unscoreable shares by publication/ingestion time-of-day, including overnight and near-open gaps, not just the successfully scored cohort.

Compare Brier score, calibration and coverage to both 0.5 and a base-rate predictor frozen from an earlier sample. Report clustered uncertainty by story and trading date; repeated headlines on one event do not inflate the sample. Freeze target, eligibility, thresholds and baseline before collection; reserve a later untouched evaluation period. Do not continuously retune against that period. Existing ten-trading-day Jev outcomes and rank-IC do not validate this new target or horizon.

No fixed two-week deadline or small sample can authorize trading. A later decision must show incremental out-of-sample predictive value, stability across independent days, and tradable economics using verified spread, fees, FX and arrival-to-order latency. If executable prices/costs are unavailable, report forecasting quality only. Lack of evidence is a no-go for signal promotion. Macro direction (CPI/Fed/etc.) is a subsequent research proposal requiring point-in-time consensus/surprise data; simple headline sentiment is insufficient and does not authorize selling VUAG.

## Phase 4: optional live cadence changes

Only after phase 0 design is implemented as D and passes adversarial tests and phase 1 has reconciled operational evidence, quote freshness is verified, and the SELL-policy gate is explicitly resolved, implement an independently reviewed exit-only path. It calls the existing mechanical rules under account ownership, with no model-directed sells, no new BUYs, no core sweep and explicit broker-state reconciliation. Shadow divergences caused by denser peak sampling are a disclosed strategy change to evaluate, not automatically a benefit.

Separately enable the second full decision review. New forecasts remain shadow-only. Code limits scheduled review starts to two per ET day and maintains daily entry capacity across all callers. Replace the once-daily cycle/catch-up/watchdog assumptions together: each expected slot has its own durable receipt, actual started/completed/failed state and bounded retry rules. A cron heartbeat alone is not proof that a session ran. An unknown or partially executed slot is reconciled, never blindly replayed; missed expired slots are reported and skipped rather than starting after close.

Keep per-cycle traces and reporting. Do not increase memory-write budgets merely because there are more cycles; bound aggregate memory proposals/admissions per ET day and preserve directive precedence. F must change core policy explicitly: only the 10:00 ET slot may initiate funding/sweep, and only while BOTH the US and London regular sessions are open. Validate both calendars even during mismatched DST weeks and UK-only holidays. The 14:00 ET slot may trade stock exposure only from available unreserved cash; it must not initiate a VUAG funding sale or sweep or queue one overnight. If insufficient cash, reject that BUY with a reason. Keep pending core orders blocking new core sends.

Replace the opaque daily funding flag with a logical funding reservation tied to a reviewed stock decision, confirmed proceeds and its intended use. Permit at most one funding operation per ET day initially; retries retain its logical ID. A definitive cancelled/expired stock decision releases unused earmarked cash, but an accepted/pending/unknown BUY keeps it reserved. Morning sweep uses only confirmed unreserved cash; unused funds released after London closes wait for the NEXT eligible morning sweep. Never silently convert funding keys into slot keys to allow repeated sales. Shared unknown-send handling also covers the sweep, which currently has no independent send-intent marker.

F's hypothesis is that a second review identifies genuinely new actionable evidence or resolves a prior cash-funded opportunity sooner without violating daily budgets. First run the 14:00 review with read-only capabilities and hypothetical decisions, no `record_cycle` sweep and no memory writes. Report incremental decision differences, turnover/cost estimates and account-risk sampling effects beside the morning-only baseline. If it adds no distinct useful decisions, retain one full review; no fixed short test proves alpha. This is an activation decision supported by observed behavior, not a platform entitlement.

## Verification and delivery gates

Implement in separate reviewable slices: (A) calendar + read-only shadow monitor; (B) incremental news observation; (C) forecast ledger/scoring research; (D) account execution ownership + daily reservation/intent migration; (E) optional live exits; (F) optional second review + slot-aware catch-up/watchdog. No single mega-PR; D must land before E/F. A-C can proceed without changing the broker write path.

Required deterministic tests (mutation-check the safety assertions): DST transition weeks, holiday/early close, duplicate/missed/delayed ticks, concurrent account callers, expired/stale owners, crash before/after send, unknown outcomes, pending orders, partial fills/re-entry, four entries across two sessions, no broker side effects from shadow/news/forecasts, atomic cursor persistence and duplicate/corrected stories, timestamp leakage, adjusted-price alignment, missing outcomes, independent story/date samples, daily trace/summary/memory limits, and startup with no new config.

Use injected fakes with a network blocker and explicit DRY_RUN+demo interlock for every test/eval. Never run a live order as validation. Node 24: app typecheck, Convex typecheck, unit suite, build and bundle smoke; behavioral evals only with working model credentials and fake broker. New populated-table fields start optional; schema PRs require production dry-run by the reviewer, never a manual production deployment. Add only the minimal run/effect receipts needed to distinguish not-run from no-action.

Before activation, verify deployed handler behavior and quota/freshness evidence with all new modes off, then shadow on. Existing daily heartbeat, cycle traces and reports must remain intact. Each live phase gets a clearly recorded activation decision and a rollback drill. Observation/news/forecast switches do not disable the established daily cycle or its exits. Disabling new execution stops new dispatches; an active operation must reach known reconciliation state before its lease is released. DRY_RUN remains the global order stop. Shadow deployments: prefer outside the US session and existing runbook windows; if an unavoidable shadow redeploy misses ticks, record a deployment gap, never fabricate observations or “no action.” Report raw expected-tick coverage and the gap count alongside any deployment-adjusted coverage so excluded gaps remain visible. Live E/F deployments require a drain flag that stops new ownership/dispatch, confirmation that active physical sends/retries are quiescent and outcomes reconciled, then deployment. Resume only after health/config/calendar checks. A deployment is not cancellation/fencing of an old worker. If drain/quiescence cannot be established, do not deploy the new execution mode; preserve the serving deployment and use the documented operator recovery path.

## Acceptance and stop conditions

- A-C are useful even if every forecast is uninformative; a valid outcome is better monitoring and a no-go on prediction-driven trading.
- Stop live activation on any unexplained broker side effect, unknown execution ownership, violated daily cap, missing/inconsistent receipts, unreconciled partial send, calendar gap, unproven/stale execution quote timestamps, resource contention that degrades the live path, or model-backed safety eval failure.
- Do not expand providers/universe/frequency to rescue weak predictions. If sources or quotas cannot support the cadence, lower it and report the measured coverage.
- Cost gate: collect incremental compute/API/model usage in shadow and present monthly GBP cost beside current account equity before any live phase; no paid feed or additional service commitment without a separate decision. Routine polls must not launch a full model turn.
